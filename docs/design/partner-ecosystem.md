# Partners, resellers and implementation providers
Status: proposed.

## Channels
Independent accountants and setup providers may charge customers for agreed implementation services. Resellers and telecom partners may distribute subscriptions through contractual arrangements. WhatsApp channel owners may run disclosed paid campaigns; measure qualified conversions rather than follower counts. No partner receives automatic access to customer Books.

## Contracts
Partner ID, agreement version, territory, permitted products, commission schedule, attribution rule, settlement currency, tax documentation and status. Commission accrues only on qualifying events and reverses under published refund terms. Separate customer service fee from Vimbai subscription. External payment/payout remains with authorized providers.

## Integration
Monetisation partner registry and commission records; referrals attribution reused; tenancy grants temporary scoped access only with customer approval; audit logs partner actions. Partner portal exposes aggregate authorized sales, not unrelated financial data. Telco integration uses versioned order and entitlement APIs with signed callbacks.

## Acceptance
Duplicate orders, partner suspension, revoked customer access, commission disputes, refunds, reconciliation and cross-territory restrictions. Pilot contracts and consumer disclosures before scaling; no unverified telco partnership claims.
