# Government and public-sector accounting
Status: proposed future capability, not a separate ledger engine.

## Scope
Ministries, departments, agencies, municipalities and public funds use organisation/Book hierarchy with jurisdiction-specific fiscal rules. Support appropriation, commitment, obligation, expenditure, revenue, grants, restricted funds, assets, procurement references, consolidation and audit. Cash/accrual and IPSAS or local frameworks require expert-approved policy packs.

## Controls
Budget availability checks precede commitments; authorized amendments preserve history; segregation of duties and delegated spending limits are effective-dated. Track fund, programme, project, economic classification and administrative unit as configurable dimensions. Public reporting uses approved redacted projections; sensitive records remain restricted.

## Integration
Extend budgeting, accounting, NPO/fund, assets, reporting and tenancy owners; cross-domain application orchestrates commitment-to-payment-record workflow. No government-specific fork or payment movement. Self-hosting and data residency supported by deployment profiles.

## Acceptance
Appropriation exhaustion, concurrent commitments, cancellation, fiscal-year rollover, interagency consolidation, audit trace, restricted access and expert-reviewed statutory reports. Pilot with a willing institution; no compliance or procurement certification assumed.
