# Strategic objectives
Status: proposed. Vimbai is intended as global, generational financial infrastructure for individuals, households, informal traders, businesses, nonprofits and institutions.

## Product principles
Make accurate bookkeeping accessible without requiring accounting expertise. Preserve user ownership, portability, offline operation, transparent financial history and jurisdictional adaptability. Hybrid commercial/public-interest ownership and licensing details require separate governance decisions; do not encode them into the ledger.

## Delivery
First prove personal, household and small-business workflows in Zimbabwe, including low-bandwidth devices and mobile-money subscription collection. Expand through configurable profiles rather than forks. Institutional and public-sector capabilities follow validated controls and independent deployment readiness.

## Measures
Track successful first Book setup, completed reconciliation, posting correctness, recovery success, retention, paid conversion, support burden and accessibility. Define targets from measured pilots rather than invented adoption projections. Collect minimal consented telemetry.

## Governance
Version policies and contracts; maintain export and migration paths; document succession, operational ownership and release authority. Product owners approve scope; financial specialists approve accounting policies; security reviewers approve isolation. Acceptance: every roadmap item maps to an objective and measurable gate.
