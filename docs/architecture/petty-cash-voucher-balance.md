# Work package 48 — Reconcile a petty cash voucher with its fund

Proposed fund accounting design, reviewed at [2c0642c](https://github.com/Benwellonedge28/Vimbai/commit/2c0642cd994674e4d455e93d524031eb6477e9a1).

## Accounting paths in scope

`rust/crates/domains/accounting/src/bookkeeping.rs` owns the working-paper fund and voucher. `create_petty_cash_fund` stores the caller's imprest and current balance. `create_petty_cash_entry` resolves accounts, posts the journal, and then remembers the voucher with the supplied fund ID. It neither resolves that ID against the fund store nor decrements the fund. `list_petty_cash_entries` uses the generic listing helper, which caps the Book read before applying the fund and date filters. This package concerns the fund and voucher endpoints registered by `rust/apps/api-server/src/accounting_book.rs`. The separately routed petty-cash service has its own commands and requires its own contract.

## Establish the fund and spend from it

Offer verified fund creation at `POST /accounting/petty-cash-funds/verified` and the corresponding records alias. Register it in the API inventory. Add a Book-scoped cash account identity and balance verification state to the accounting fund record. On creation, obtain the selected account through `subsidiary_journals::account_for_purpose`, restricted to a cash asset held in Book currency, and save its stable chart identity. Require a positive imprest and an opening balance between zero and the imprest. A zero opening balance is valid. A positive opening balance needs an authorized opening or replenishment evidence reference with the same fund allocation and amount. Creating the working-paper fund itself records neither a receipt of cash nor a ledger entry. A legacy fund stays visible before its first verification. Enable the exact-balance guarantee per fund after affirmative migration. Keep the legacy creation signature during the ADR-004 transition and label its balances as unverified. Both voucher writers must share the fund-state check, so an older client cannot write around the verified fund balance.

A voucher uses `(owner scope, Book, fund ID, voucher number)` as its business key. Hold the fund version and voucher key inside the scoped posting transaction described in [package 22](opening-balance-receipts.md). If a voucher already exists, compare its canonical date, amount, expense account, payee and supporting references. Return the existing row for a match and return 409 for a changed submission. For a new voucher, require a verified fund, an open voucher date and positive exact `Money` in Book currency. The assigned cash account supplies the credit and the explicit eligible expense account supplies the debit. An explicitly supplied petty-cash account must agree with the fund. Reject a debit to the fund account and refuse an amount above the verified available balance. Use `ledger_book::create_entry` for the journal, save the voucher with its journal ID, and subtract the exact credited amount from the fund balance in one commit. Store the before and after balance and increment the fund version with the voucher. A stale writer reloads the fund and re-evaluates the same voucher key.

## Read, refuse and repair

Keep the successful vouchers and funds in their existing response shapes. Add verification state to fund reads. Apply scope, fund and date predicates before pagination for voucher reads, with date, creation time and ID giving a stable order for equal dates. `accounting_book.rs` keeps both existing verbs on each path and maps domain errors to the existing envelope. For a missing fund say “Choose a petty cash fund from this Book.” For insufficient cash, say “This voucher exceeds the fund’s verified cash balance.” A duplicate with changed details says “This voucher number has already been recorded for this fund. Open the original voucher.” For a missing opening or unmatched journals say “Verify this fund’s opening cash and vouchers before recording another voucher.” A failed write is retryable with the same voucher number.

During upgrade, keep every fund, voucher and posting ID. Reconstruct a verified balance from authorized opening or replenishment evidence and the complete history of uniquely linked vouchers. Compare each voucher amount with its journal credit to the selected cash account and record the reviewer, starting balance, reviewed journal IDs and resulting balance. Where a journal has no unique voucher, preserve both the reported balance and the discrepancy for review. A repair links evidence or produces an authorized accounting correction through the existing reversal path. It cannot edit the posted journal or silently seed the balance from the stale working paper.

## Acceptance evidence

- Add `petty_cash_second_concurrent_voucher_cannot_overspend` to the accounting provider integration target specified in [package 47](subsidiary-journal-posting-receipts.md). Start two distinct vouchers with a combined amount above available cash and check exactly one debit and credit and one fund delta on both providers.
- Add `petty_cash_voucher_fault_keeps_fund_and_ledger_at_previous_version` to the same suite. Fail the voucher insert and fund update in separate cases and inspect both after restart.
- In `accounting_end_to_end_http.rs`, add `petty_cash_voucher_replays_within_fund_and_refuses_wrong_book`. Cover matching and changed submissions and a reused voucher number in a different authorized fund.
- Add `legacy_fund_with_unmatched_voucher_stays_unverified` beside the bookkeeping tests, plus an HTTP list test with unrelated vouchers ahead of the requested fund.

The package is ready when an auditor can derive the displayed balance from reviewed opening cash and voucher journal credits, and the paid voucher and balance change always appear together.
