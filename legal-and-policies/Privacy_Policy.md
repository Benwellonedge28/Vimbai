# Vimbai Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 2026

## 1. Zero-Trust Privacy Model
Vimbai operates on a zero-trust, privacy-by-design architecture. We believe your financial data belongs to you. To enforce this, Vimbai encrypts your sensitive financial information on your device before it is ever transmitted to our servers.

## 2. What We Cannot See
Because of end-to-end encryption, Vimbai **cannot access, read, or share**:
- Your income or revenue details
- Your expenses or transaction history
- Your financial reports or budgets
- Your private documents, receipts, or notes

## 3. What We Collect and Store
To operate the service, we collect and store only the minimum necessary data:
- **Account Identity:** Email address or cryptographic identifier to manage your account.
- **Subscription Status:** Information necessary to manage your chosen plan (Free, Family, Enterprise, etc.).
- **Authentication Information:** Cryptographic proofs to verify your identity (we do not store raw biometrics).
- **Encrypted Data Blobs:** Opaque, encrypted files for cloud synchronization. We hold the ciphertext; you hold the keys.
- **Operational Telemetry:** Anonymized metrics such as crash reports, storage usage, and aggregated feature usage.

## 4. Third-Party Storage (Bring Your Own Storage)
You have the right to store your encrypted Vimbai backups on external providers (e.g., Google Drive, OneDrive, private servers). Vimbai acts as the encryption and software layer; the storage provider only receives the encrypted `.vmb` file.

## 5. Data Deletion
You can delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all your encrypted blobs and account metadata are permanently purged from our servers within 30 days.

---
*Vimbai will never sell your data. We cannot sell what we cannot read.*
