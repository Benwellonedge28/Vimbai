# Vimbai Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## 1. Uptime Commitment
Vimbai guarantees a 99.9% Monthly Uptime Percentage for Business, Enterprise, and Government plans.

## 2. Offline-First Resilience
Because Vimbai is designed with an offline-first, local-database architecture, temporary server outages will not prevent you from viewing or entering data on your device. Synchronization will resume automatically once connectivity is restored.
