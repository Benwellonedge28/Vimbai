# Vimbai Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

## 1. Scope
This DPA applies to Enterprise and Business customers. It outlines how Vimbai processes data on behalf of the organization.

## 2. Processing Instructions
Vimbai processes data exclusively to provide the financial management software. Because Vimbai employs end-to-end encryption for financial records, Vimbai acts as a "blind" processor for that specific data category.

## 3. Sub-processors
Vimbai uses third-party infrastructure (e.g., AWS, GCP) to host the encrypted blobs. These sub-processors have no access to the decryption keys and cannot read the data.
